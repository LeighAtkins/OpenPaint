A drawing companion for ChatGPT

From a sofa photo.
To a useful drawing.

Place connected measurement lines on your original photos. Follow upholstery seams, compare multiple angles, and make a clear document for the person holding the tape measure.

Open SofaPaintConnect in ChatGPT

01 / See the shape

ChatGPT identifies a sofa, armchair, sectional, or cushion and chooses the closest measurement guide.

02 / Draw the surface

Editable lines connect visible seams and useful boundaries across your front, side, and back photos.

03 / Take the measure

Download a printable or fillable PDF. Enter your own measurements in the matching fields.

Connect your drawing companion

If your ChatGPT account supports custom MCP connections, add the public server below with authentication set to none. The public directory listing is being prepared; this page does not claim directory approval.

https://sofapaint-mcp.sofapaint-api.workers.dev/public/mcp

Then upload your sofa photos and ask: “Draw seam-to-seam measurements over these photos, review each view, and give me a fillable PDF in centimetres.”

No account is required by SofaPaint. Temporary project links expire after 24 hours. Download anything you want to keep. Drawings help explain where to measure; they do not infer physical dimensions from photos.
