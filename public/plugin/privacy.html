Privacy

This policy covers the SofaPaint measurement connector and its temporary drawing drafts.

What you choose to send

When you use the connector, ChatGPT sends selected image download links, furniture observations, placement coordinates, and drawing instructions to SofaPaint. SofaPaint downloads those selected photos and stores them with the draft, lines, previews, and PDF exports to provide the drawing service.

Storage and expiry

Cloudflare processes the connector requests and stores temporary draft files in R2. Project access expires 24 hours after creation. An hourly cleanup removes expired drafts and associated photos, previews, and PDFs; outages can delay physical deletion. Anyone holding a private project link can access that draft until expiry, so treat it as private.

Hosting and other providers

The editor and these pages are hosted on Vercel. Opening a draft in the editor fetches its photos and drawing data from Cloudflare. OpenAI processes your ChatGPT conversation under its own policies. SofaPaint does not send these photos to a separate paid AI model for analysis. Infrastructure providers may process connection information, including IP addresses and technical service records.

Your choices

Only upload photos you are entitled to share. Download your files before expiry. For privacy questions or an early deletion request, contact leigh.atkins7@gmail.com. Include the project identifier, but avoid emailing a private access link unless needed to verify your request.
